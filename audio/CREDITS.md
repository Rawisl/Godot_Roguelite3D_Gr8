## Sound effects (`audio/sfx/`)

Every effect is a 16-bit 44.1 kHz .wav, loudness-normalized toward -12 LUFS with the true peak
held at -1 dBTP (a limiter takes at most 6 dB off the peaks, so very short clicks and taps stay
a little under -12 rather than distort).
